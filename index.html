About Breakin' Bread

Breakin' Bread Bakery is a cozy neighborhood bakery in Union built around fresh bread, welcoming service, and the kind of food that keeps people coming back. Whether you are stopping in for breakfast, specialty coffee, lunch, pastries, or a custom order, the goal is simple: serve food that feels fresh, comforting, and worth sharing.

We bake fresh bread daily, offer made-to-order favorites throughout the day, and create a warm local spot where customers can grab something quick or take their time. From everyday bakery staples to custom orders and specialty drinks, Breakin' Bread brings together coffee, bread, and breakfast in one neighborhood destination.
